# 000175: fix/spurious-terminal-input

## Intent

Fix spurious escape sequences and random characters (`>|Triage 0.3.0...65;4;6;18;22;52c...`, `0;61;459M0;61;459m`, and `35;55;29M35;54;29M...`) being injected into normal shell sessions after daemon reloads or after a child TUI/agent exits without disabling mouse tracking, and fix text overwriting upper lines as text is typed or streamed (`devlog/plans/000175-01-fix-spurious-terminal-input.md`).

## Issues

- `2026-10-26T20:33-0700` Omitting `\r\n` on soft-wrapped lines in `scrollback::encode_line` and rebuilding `.slog` journals via `replay_tail_with_scrollback_rebuild` on handover caused two regressions in scrollback formatting: (1) full-width table/diff rows that touched the right margin had `last_cell_was_wrapped() == true` and were glued onto the same line on wider terminals, and (2) sessions whose `.tlog` spanned multiple historical terminal widths or alternate-screen `CHA` (`\x1b[108G`) cursor jumps had their `.slog` rebuilt at a single narrower `TIOCGWINSZ` width (`62` cols), clamping cursor jumps and scrolling synthetic wrapped rows into `.slog`.
