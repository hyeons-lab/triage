## Agent

## Intent
Migrate `flutter/triage_client` from the brittle `xterm 4.0.0` git fork to `xterm2` (`^5.4.0`), retire the `xterm` `dependency_overrides` block and redundant client-side parser/mouse workarounds that `xterm2` implements natively, and verify parity across scrollback clearing, reflow, scroll anchoring, selection, and mouse reporting.

## Decisions
- 2026-11-04T18:31-0800 Replace `package:xterm` with `package:xterm2`: `xterm2` (`^5.4.0`) already passed the Phase -1 benchmark gate (`devlog/000166-spike-term-parse-bench.md`, 2.6-3.7x faster parse throughput and within a single 16.7ms frame on Pixel 10 Pro Fold for 256 KiB replay) and includes upstream implementations for `IndexAwareCircularBuffer.trimStart` (`_absoluteStartIndex` advance + line detach), `replaceWith` rotation reset, `Buffer.resize` cursor-anchor tracking across width reflow, `CSI s`/`CSI u`, `CSI Ps Z` (`CBT`) and `CSI Ps I` (`CHT`), DEC Mode 2026 Synchronized Output, `modifyOtherKeys`, Kitty keyboard protocol, unshifted mouse wheel button IDs (`64/65/66/67`), and viewport-relative mouse reporting (`_getViewportCellOffset`).
- 2026-11-04T18:31-0800 Simplify `writeToXterm` and mouse wheel reporting: delegate `writeToXterm` directly to `terminal.write(data)` now that `xterm2` handles `CBT` and `CHT` natively in `_csiHandlers`, and route `_sendWheelStep` through `_terminal.mouseInput` with `TerminalMouseButton.wheelUp`/`wheelDown` instead of manually formatting SGR/URXVT/normal mouse wheel escape sequences or rewriting `68/69` to `64/65` in `_onTerminalOutput`.
- 2026-11-04T21:02-0800 Strip Mode 2026 markers in `writeToXterm` and batch history replay notifications: `TerminalStore` already buffers Mode 2026 frames and flushes open frames on its 50ms idle watchdog, 100ms live-flush interval, or at the end of history replay. Forwarding `\x1b[?2026h` and `\x1b[?2026l` to `xt.Terminal.write` caused `xterm2` to allocate its own 150ms watchdog timer per frame and suppress `terminal.notifyListeners()` for 150ms whenever `TerminalStore` flushed an open frame. Stripping those markers in `writeToXterm` while wrapping `SessionVm._flushPendingHistory` in `terminal.setSynchronizedUpdateMode(true)` eliminates timer churn and coalesces all replay writes into a single listener notification.
- 2026-11-05T16:49-0800 Back native Flutter terminal sessions with `crates/triage-term` (`tattoy-wezterm-term`) via `dart:ffi`: rather than maintaining a bespoke CustomPainter alongside a new VT state machine, `TriageRustTerminal` subclasses `xt.Terminal`, feeds UTF-8 bytes to `tattoy-wezterm-term` in Rust, and copies only dirty rows (`line.changed_since(last_synced_seqno)`) directly into `BufferLine.data`'s 4-word cell layout. Replaying `explicit_trimmed_top` (`clearScrollback`/`trimStart`) and `pushed_bottom` (`lines.push`) preserves `BufferLine` object identity across scrollback eviction, `ESC[3J`, and width reflow so `RenderTerminal`, `TerminalScrollAnchor`, and `TerminalController` selection work unmodified, while web and unbuilt environments fall back to `xt.Terminal`.

## Issues
- 2026-11-05T19:12-0800 UI isolate freeze in `TriageRustTerminal._syncFromRust` on mobile resize: when narrowing width from 80 to 44 columns at scrollback capacity (`maxLines = 1000`), `tattoy-wezterm-term`'s `Screen::rewrap_lines` expanded wrapped scrollback rows past `max_lines`; and when shrinking height on the alternate screen (e.g. from 24 rows to 15 rows when the soft keyboard opened) with a non-whitespace bottom status bar, `Screen::resize` left `screen.lines.len() == 24` while `altBuffer.lines.maxLength` was `15`. In both cases `totalLines > buf.lines.maxLength`, causing `while (buf.lines.length < totalLines) { buf.lines.push(...); }` to spin forever because `IndexAwareCircularBuffer.push` evicts at `maxLength` without increasing `length`. Resolved by clamping `total_lines` to `physical_rows` on the alternate screen and `self.max_lines.max(physical_rows)` on the primary screen in `TriageTermEngine::sync()`, ensuring `buf.lines.maxLength >= max(totalLines, buf.viewHeight)` in `_syncFromRust`, batching Rust syncs across `setSynchronizedUpdateMode(true)`, and replacing `Screen::lines_in_phys_range` with zero-clone `Screen::for_each_phys_line`.
