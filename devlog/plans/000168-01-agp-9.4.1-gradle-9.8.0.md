# 000168-01: Upgrade AGP to 9.4.1 and Gradle Wrapper to 9.8.0

## Thinking

Android builds in `flutter/triage_client/android` use AGP `8.11.1` and Gradle `8.14`. Bumping to AGP `9.4.1` and Gradle `9.8.0` keeps the Android toolchain current and adds SHA-256 integrity verification (`distributionSha256Sum`) plus transient download retry handling (`retries=3`, `retryBackOffMs=500`) to the Gradle Wrapper configuration.

## Plan

1. Update `com.android.application` in `flutter/triage_client/android/settings.gradle.kts` to `9.4.1`.
2. Update `flutter/triage_client/android/gradle/wrapper/gradle-wrapper.properties` to `gradle-9.8.0-all.zip` with pinned `distributionSha256Sum`, `validateDistributionUrl=true`, and `retries=3`.
3. Record changes and decisions in `devlog/000168-chore-agp-9.4.1-gradle-9.8.0.md`.
